Who I am

I'm Thamer SASSI, a first-year Computer Science student specialising in Artificial Intelligence at the West University of Timișoara, Romania.

My interests sit at the intersection of technology, philosophy, and digital freedom. I'm drawn to questions about what software means for society, how AI reshapes our understanding of consciousness, and whether reality itself might be something other than it appears.

Outside of coursework I write — mostly as a way of thinking out loud. These articles are honest attempts to work through ideas I find worth sharing.
